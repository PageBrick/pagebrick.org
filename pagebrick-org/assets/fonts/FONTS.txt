Fonts of the pagebrick.org theme (both under the SIL Open Font License, see OFL.txt)

inter-tight.woff2
  Inter Tight, variable, Latin: as it comes from its authors.

newsreader.woff2
  Newsreader, variable, Latin. Narrowed to what the theme uses: weights 300 to 500 (the titles and the big numbers are weight 300) and optical sizes 24 to 72
  (they are drawn at 24 px and more). 132 KB became 86 KB. Made with fontTools:
    python -m fontTools.varLib.instancer newsreader-original.woff2 wght=300:500 opsz=24:72 -o newsreader.woff2
